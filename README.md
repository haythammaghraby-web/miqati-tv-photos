# Miqati TV screensaver photos

Photos shown by the screensaver of Miqati on Android TV / Google TV.
The app reads `index.json` and downloads the files in `photos/` once, keeping them on the television.
To add or remove a photo: change `photos/` and `index.json`; televisions pick it up within 12 hours.

All photos are from Pexels (free for commercial use, no attribution required); see SOURCES.md.
